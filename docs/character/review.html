Embertrail / the fox, refined

Your supplied fox artwork is the visual base. Twenty-seven original sprites now cover idle, walking, running, dash, the airborne arc, landing, damage and collapse. The expansion pass replaced the whole-image tilts that previously stood in for jumping, falling, landing, hurt and death with separately drawn poses from the same sheet, and split the arc into ascent, apex and descent. Differently sized crops share a consistent body/foot anchor. Original source files remain untouched.
